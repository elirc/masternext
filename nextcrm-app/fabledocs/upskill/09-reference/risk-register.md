## How to use this

- **Contributors**: items 1, 4, 8, 13 map directly to [good-first-tickets](../06-contribution-practice/01-good-first-tickets.md); 2, 3, 7, 10 to [mid/senior work](../06-contribution-practice/03-senior-build-projects.md).
- **Interviewees**: items 1, 3, 4, 6 are your strongest "risk I found" stories — but always deliver them with the confidence label attached ("confirmed" vs "I'd want to verify X").
- **Reviewers**: this doubles as the pre-merge checklist's rationale — [05/05-security-checklist](../05-quality-engineering/05-security-checklist.md).

Every "investigate" item is an invitation, not a claim. Verifying one (especially #10 and #16) and reporting it properly per [maintainer communication](../07-career-and-collaboration/03-maintainer-communication.md) is itself an excellent contribution.
